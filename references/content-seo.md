# Content SEO

Contents: search intent, keyword and topic research, topical coverage, content quality and E-E-A-T, YMYL, editorial standards, content types by site type, AI-assisted content, refreshing and pruning, authority and link earning, measurement, checklist.

## 1. Search intent first

For each target query decide the dominant intent, then match it:

| Intent | Examples | Page type |
|---|---|---|
| Informational | what is, how to, guide | Article, guide, glossary |
| Commercial investigation | best, vs, review, alternatives | Comparison, review, buyer's guide |
| Transactional | buy, price, order, book | Product, service, booking page |
| Navigational | brand or site name | Homepage, login, contact |
| Local | near me, in [city] | Location page, GBP, directory |

Review the live SERP: which formats and angles rank (lists, videos, forums, products, maps)? Match the format, then add something better or different. Never copy competitors' text.

## 2. Keyword and topic research (no paid tools required)

Sources: Search Console queries, Google autocomplete, related searches, People Also Ask, Google Trends (relative interest), Google Keyword Planner ranges if the owner has an Ads account, customer questions, support tickets, social and forum discussions, competitor sitemaps and SERP review.

- Group keywords by topic and intent, not by individual phrase. One page per intent cluster.
- Record source and date for each keyword. Never publish volume numbers that were not obtained from a named tool; if a tool is unavailable, say volume is unknown.
- Prefer topics where the site has first-hand experience or data.
- For Nepal, include English, Nepali and Roman Nepali variants (see `local-seo-nepal.md`).

## 3. Topical coverage

- Build clusters: a pillar page covering a broad topic and supporting pages for subtopics and questions, interlinked with descriptive anchors.
- Map the customer journey: awareness, comparison, decision, post-purchase support.
- Find gaps by comparing the site's pages with queries it already gets impressions for but ranks poorly, and with questions customers ask.
- Consolidate overlapping pages that compete for the same intent (keyword cannibalization): merge, redirect with 301, update internal links.

## 4. Content quality and E-E-A-T

Google's guidance emphasizes people-first content. E-E-A-T (experience, expertise, authoritativeness, trustworthiness) is a framework used by quality raters and is not a single ranking factor.

- **Experience:** first-hand use, testing, photos, process, local knowledge.
- **Expertise:** accurate depth from people qualified on the topic, with bylines and bios.
- **Authoritativeness:** recognition by others in the field (citations, mentions, links, memberships).
- **Trust:** accuracy, transparency (who runs the site, contact details, editorial and correction policy), secure site, fair advertising and affiliate disclosure.

Practical signals: named authors with real bios, publication and update dates, cited primary sources, original images and data, clear business ownership, reachable contact methods, honest reviews methodology.

## 5. YMYL topics

Health, finance, legal, safety and civic information have a higher bar. Use qualified authors or reviewers, cite official sources, show review dates, avoid exaggerated claims, and add appropriate disclaimers (not as a substitute for accuracy). For Nepal, cite official bodies (relevant ministries, Nepal Rastra Bank for monetary and banking matters, Inland Revenue Department for tax) and state when rules may change.

## 6. Editorial standards

- Define a style guide: tone, reading level for the audience, units and formats (NPR, dates in AD with BS where helpful), naming conventions.
- Fact-check claims and numbers, store sources, and review before publish.
- Show corrections openly.
- Disclose sponsored content and affiliate links (`rel="sponsored"` for paid and affiliate links).
- Keep a content calendar tied to real demand, not volume of posts.

## 7. Content by site type

- **Business and service:** service pages, case studies, process, pricing guidance, location pages, FAQs from customers, team bios.
- **Blog and news:** original reporting or analysis, clear bylines, dates, corrections, topic hubs, `NewsArticle` or `Article` markup, News publisher transparency (about, ownership, editorial policy). Check Google News and Top stories requirements if relevant.
- **Education:** program and admissions pages, accurate fees and dates, outcomes with sources, faculty profiles, guides for students, course and event markup where eligible.
- **Portfolio:** project pages with context, role, process, results; about page with specific expertise; contact; avoid image-only pages.
- **Government and nonprofit:** service and procedure pages written in plain language (Nepali and English), forms and documents in accessible HTML or tagged PDFs, up-to-date notices, transparency and financial reports, donation transparency, contact details for offices.
- **Real estate, hospitality, tourism:** listings with accurate facts and photos, area guides, seasonal and route content, local trust signals (see `local-seo-nepal.md`).
- **Ecommerce and SaaS:** see `ecommerce-seo.md` and `saas-seo.md`.

## 8. AI-assisted content

- Google does not penalize content solely because AI helped produce it; it penalizes content created mainly to manipulate rankings, including scaled low-value content.
- Use AI for outlines, editing, summarizing and drafts, then add human experience, verify facts, and edit. Disclose AI use where it matters to readers.
- Never publish unreviewed machine translations or hallucination-prone text on factual topics.
- Never fabricate authors, quotes, reviews, statistics or sources.

## 9. Refresh and prune

- Refresh pages with declining clicks and still-valid intent: update facts, examples, screenshots, internal links and the visible "last updated" date when changes are real.
- Merge or redirect thin, outdated or duplicate pages. Delete (404 or 410) only when no traffic, links or value remain.
- Keep a log: URL, action, date, expected outcome, review date.

## 10. Authority and link earning

- Earn links by publishing original research, tools, data, local guides, expert commentary, and by genuine PR and partnerships.
- Reach out only to relevant sites with something useful; no mass link buying, link exchanges at scale, private blog networks or paid links without `rel="sponsored"`.
- Disavow only for confirmed manipulative links the site is responsible for. It is rarely necessary.
- Unlinked brand mentions: ask politely for attribution.

## 11. Measurement

- Search Console: impressions, clicks, average position by page and query group, over a comparable period. Position is an average; do not report single numbers as fixed rankings.
- Analytics: engagement, conversions, assisted conversions.
- Content ROI: pages with conversions vs cost to produce.
- Always compare like for like (same date range, seasonality, device, country).

## 12. Checklist

- [ ] Each page has one clear intent and a target query cluster
- [ ] Priorities backed by a named data source
- [ ] Authors, dates, sources and about/contact information visible
- [ ] YMYL pages reviewed by qualified people and cite official sources
- [ ] Cannibalization identified and resolved
- [ ] Refresh and prune plan with owners and dates
- [ ] No scaled low-value content; AI-assisted drafts reviewed
- [ ] Link and mention strategy is editorial, not purchased
