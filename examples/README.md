# Example audits

These examples are fictional teaching materials. They do not contain measured traffic, rankings, real businesses, or real customer data. Use them to understand the report structure and evidence standard; do not copy their claims into a real audit.

- `fictional-local-business-audit.md`: a Nepal service business example.
- `fictional-ecommerce-audit.md`: an online shop example.

For a real audit, label every finding as **Verified** or **Assumption**, cite the page, file, or command output used as evidence, and do not invent metrics.
