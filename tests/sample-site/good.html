Dental Clinic in Pokhara

This is a fictional sample page. It describes dental check-ups, cleaning, fillings and braces for adults and children, with appointments available six days a week. The clinic is open Sunday to Friday from 9 am to 5 pm and closed on Saturdays. Bring any previous dental records and a list of medicines you take. For urgent pain, call the number on the contact page and we will try to see you the same day. Prices are listed in NPR on the pricing page and are confirmed before treatment begins. We explain each step in English or Nepali so you can decide what is right for you. Parking is available beside the building and a ramp provides step-free access for wheelchairs and prams. First visits take about forty minutes and include an examination, a short discussion about your goals, and a written treatment plan that you can take home.
